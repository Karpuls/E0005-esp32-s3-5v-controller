## Board
- **4 layers, 1.6 mm finished, 1 oz outer / 0.5 oz inner**, stackup **JLC04161H-3313**:
  - L1 0.035;
  - prepreg 3313 0.0994;
  - L2 0.0152;
  - core 1.265;
  - L3 0.0152;
  - prepreg 0.0994;
  - L4 0.035 mm.
- Layer use:
  - **F.Cu**: parts, most signals, power pours.
  - **In1**: solid GND, no tracks.
  - **In2**: V5 plane (west column and north port band), 3V3 plane, short slow-signal lanes.
  - **B.Cu**: signals, GND pour, holder lugs, B.Cu V5 strap, MID pours.
- **125 × 73 mm**, R3 corners, 4 × M3 holes (Ø3.2 mm plated, 4 mm corner inset).
- **Vias are tented** (board setting: tenting front and back), so there are no mask openings on vias. The only exceptions are the EP thermal vias inside U2's and U15's exposed pads.
- Vias 0.6/0.3 mm. Minimum track 0.15 mm (signals). JLC clearance rules plus the owner's via-to-pad rule are in `E0005_controller.kicad_dru`.
- Drills:
  - Plated, 701:
    - 685 × 0.30 (vias);
    - 4 × 0.60 oval slots (J11 USB-C shell);
    - 4 × 0.80 (NTC leads);
    - 4 × 1.30 (holder lugs);
    - 4 × 3.20 (M3).
  - **Non-plated, 8:**
    - 2 × 0.65 (USB-C pegs);
    - 2 × 2.40 and 4 × 3.60 (Keystone 1121 holder locating pegs).
- **USB D+/D−:** 90 Ω differential, F.Cu over In1 GND. W 0.153 mm, S 0.20 mm, coplanar with a 0.30 mm gap to the pour (see Impedance control).
- Board checks (`reports/`):
  - **DRC: 0 errors, 0 unconnected, 0 schematic-parity issues** (all severities, zones refilled). The **only** violation is the accepted U1 `lib_footprint_mismatch` warning (local override of the ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 footprint).
  - **ERC: 0 violations** (all severities).

## JLC order settings
- Layers **4**; thickness **1.6 mm**; outer copper **1 oz**, inner **0.5 oz**.
- **Impedance control: yes, stackup JLC04161H-3313.**
- Via covering: **tented**.
- Surface finish **ENIG** (as E0003; the board file's stackup finish is ENIG too). Any mask colour (E0003: any).
- Size 125 × 73 mm. Material FR-4, TG as standard.
- Remove the order number, or specify its location. There is room on the bottom between the holders.
- Bottom side: through-hole parts only (BH1/BH2 holders, RT1/RT2 NTCs), so B.Paste is empty. **Hand assembly with a stencil** (spec: Build). The holders and NTCs are hand-soldered.

### Impedance control
**90 Ω differential** for the **USB_DP / USB_DN** pair:
- on **L1 (F.Cu)**, referenced to **L2 (In1, solid GND)**;
- **W 0.153 mm / S 0.20 mm**, coplanar with a **0.30 mm gap to the pour**;
- stackup **JLC04161H-3313** (L1–L2 prepreg 3313, 0.0994 mm).

The width comes from JLC's impedance calculator (owner, 2026-10-28): 6.02 mil coplanar at S 0.20 mm. The pair has no layer changes; only the J11 B6/B7 contacts are tied over by two small vias.

## Gerber render check (summary of [docs/gerber_review.md](docs/gerber_review.md))
- **Copper:** the 4 layers match the board renders. **In1 is solid GND**: its only voids are antipads, hole clearances and the edge pullback. The **antenna keep-out has 0 copper** on all layers.
- **Mask:** openings on pads only. 685 vias are tented, apart from the EP vias and findings F-1/F-2 from the preliminary run (fixed in routing; see the gerber review).
- **Silk:** legible. **"5V" is at every port J1–J5** ("5V Jn E0001" and "5V J5 E0002"). Nothing on pads. The bottom silk is mirrored correctly.
- **Outline:** 125.0 × 73.0 mm with R3 corners. J11's shell slots are plated 0.6 mm ovals.
- **Drills:** the holder pegs (Ø3.6 / Ø2.4) and USB-C pegs are NPTH; M3, lugs and NTCs are plated.

The STEP is otherwise complete: board, the other parts, and the holders with cells.
