## Deliverables
1. **One deterministic, re-runnable export script** in `build/scripts/` (e.g. `fab_export.sh` or `.py`) that writes everything into `fab/E0005_revA/` from the committed files. Running it twice gives the same output, apart from timestamps.
   - **ERC report** (`sch erc --severity-all`) and **DRC report** (`pcb drc --severity-all --schematic-parity --refill-zones`), in `reports/`. The DRC must show 0 errors, 0 unconnected and 0 parity, with the U1 `lib_footprint_mismatch` as the only exception; say so in the README.
   - **JLC-format gerbers + Excellon drill**, in `gerbers/` plus a zip `E0005_revA_gerbers_JLC.zip`. Follow JLC's KiCad settings: Protel extensions, no X2 if JLC advises against it, all 4 copper layers, both masks, silks and pastes, Edge.Cuts; PTH and NPTH drill files with a drill map. **Tented vias** (no mask openings on vias). Refill zones before plotting.
   - **Position file in JLC CPL format**, top and bottom (Designator, Mid X, Mid Y, Layer, Rotation), in mm. Also the BOM in JLC format if it's easy; the DigiKey BOM is the primary one.
   - **DigiKey BOM:** regenerate `bom/E0005_digikey_bom.csv` with `build/scripts/make_bom.py` and copy it in as `E0005_revA_BOM.csv` with a `BOM_README.md` like E0003's.
   - **Board STEP** in `3d/` (full board with components).
   - **Assembly drawings (PDF)**, top and bottom, in `assembly/`: references and outlines, as E0003.
2. **Gerber review with the `kicad-gerbers` skill:** render every layer **from the gerber files themselves** (not from the board) and look at each image. Check, and write down in `fab/E0005_revA/docs/gerber_review.md` with images:
   - copper on each layer matches the board renders in `docs/images/routing_*.png`;
   - **In1 is solid GND** (only clearances around through-holes);
   - the **antenna keep-out is clear** of copper on all layers;
   - mask openings on pads only, with vias tented;
   - silkscreen legible, nothing on pads, and **"5V" next to every port J1–J5**;
   - board outline 125 × 73 mm with R3 corners, and any slots (J11 shell);
   - drill sizes, with the **holder pegs (Ø3.6 / Ø2.4) as NPTH**, the M3 holes and the lug holes as expected.
3. **JLC impedance check:** with the backplane browser (`preview_*` tools), try JLC's impedance calculator (jlcpcb.com, impedance calculator, stackup **JLC04161H-3313**, 4 layers 1.6 mm). Confirm the differential impedance of **W 0.19 mm / S 0.20 mm on L1 over L2** is 90 Ω ± 10 %, or read the width and gap it gives for 90 Ω. Record the result with a screenshot path. If the site can't be reached or needs a login, leave it as an order-time item in `PREORDER.md`.
4. **`fab/E0005_revA/README.md`** with the order settings, as E0003's: 4 layers, 1.6 mm, JLC04161H-3313 with impedance control, 1 oz outer / 0.5 oz inner, tented vias, surface finish and colour as E0003, size 125 × 73 mm, file list, DRC/ERC status, assembly notes.
   **`fab/E0005_revA/PREORDER.md`** with:
   - **BQ25887RGER** (cut tape 296-53517-1-ND) and **TPS56637RPAR**: confirm the cut-tape DigiKey part number at order time;
   - **P50B test fit in the Keystone 1121** (flat top vs polarizing ribs);
   - the **JLC impedance check** (if not closed above);
   - **bench checks at bring-up**: O-1 TPS56637 load-step stability (FF 100 pF, 220 pF option), O-2b TPS56637 temperature with the SW discharge path active (USB only, no battery), O-4 USB-unplug V5 dip at full load and U_IN temperature in current limit;
   - the LCSC / Adafruit / nkon parts from `bom/notes.md` (GH connectors, EYESPI cable, cells).
5. **Project `README.md`** at the repo root, modelled on E0003's: what the board is, key specs table (125 × 73 mm, 4 layers, JLC04161H-3313, ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N16R2, 2S P50B, 5 V-only USB-C without PD, BQ25887 + TPS56637 + 6 × TPS259470A), features and interfaces, power architecture in short, copper layer images, 3D renders (top, bottom), repository layout, status and open items. Use the existing renders in `docs/images/` or make fresh ones.
6. **Commit and push** at milestones (script + outputs, gerber review, READMEs). Before reporting, check that `git log origin/main -1` is your commit, then run DRC (`--severity-all --schematic-parity --refill-zones`) on a fresh `git archive origin/main` copy and quote only those numbers.

## Owner house rules (permanent)
- No test points. Bring-up is through USB and component pads.
- No vias on or under pads (≥ 0.15 mm via-to-pad copper gap; EP thermal vias exempt).
- All tracks at multiples of 45°.
- DigiKey-preferred full BOM, common parts as defaults (see E0003 `bom/` common-parts list).
- JLC 4-layer impedance stackup (90 Ω USB pair), JLC design rules + no-via-on-pad rule in `.kicad_dru`.
- Commit at every milestone.
